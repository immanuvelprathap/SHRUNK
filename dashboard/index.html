On-Premise Multi-Layer Architecture

How SHRUNK keeps PHI inside the clinic

🔐 On-premise by design. Each layer runs on clinic edge hardware. Patient data is encrypted at rest, retrieved locally, and never pooled in a central cloud.

1. Privacy & Compliance Active

Consent gating, audit logging, de-identification, and institutional access controls. PHI stays sealed inside the clinic.

2. Federated Retrieval Active

Local FAISS + sentence-transformers retrieve patient, similar-patient, and guideline notes. Cross-clinic sharing only transfers model updates.

3. Clinical Routing Active

Classifies queries and escalates high-risk cases to the clinician.

4. Decision Support Active

Evidence-based recommendations from Apertus, grounded in retrieved notes and clinical guidelines.

5. Knowledge Distillation Active

Teacher (Apertus 8B/70B) fine-tunes a small student (SmolLM) for safe, edge-deployable inference.

6. Edge Deployment Active

Resource-aware model selection: local Apertus, GGUF, or secure Kaggle 70B offloading — retrieval and routing remain local.
