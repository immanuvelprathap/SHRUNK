SHRUNK

Federated RAG-based edge clinical intelligence system | mental health

Powered by dFRAG-Patient

🎯 The Mission: built to assist mental health clinicians drowning in fragmented contexts.

Our core USPs:

  1. Adaptive clinical workflow for visibility of the patient black-box problem.
  2. A psychiatry-adapted teacher built on top of sovereign LLM ("Apertus") via LoRA adaptor that provides a context-aware, mental-health curated, evidence-based ecosystem.
  3. Distilled Student's insights across other interconnected clinics with PHI sealed node-sharing - SHRUNK'ed at scarce-resourced clinics.

Hence, clinical intelligence moves while patient data never does.

🧠 The Technology: a domain-specific, Swiss sovereign open-weights LLM agent, fine-tuned and self-training on mental-health data, distils to a shrunk Student into an edge model. That supports native multilingual requirements in French, German, Italian and Swiss dialects.

🛡️ The Wedge: the Swiss National EHR and revFADP make centralized pools a legal crisis. SHRUNK provides an ecosystem where the legal crisis stays sealed at the edge of the patient's medium of access, while the necessary diagnosis conveyed remains EHR / E-GD compliant.

The Problem
Mental health clinicians drown in fragmented contexts. Doctors at each node only see local data, so the patient is a black box between visits and doctor-to-doctor collaboration is limited. Patients miss a continuous longitudinal view for triage, diagnosis and treatment planning.

The Problem, the patient is a black box

Doctors at each node work in isolation with fragmented local data, so the patient is a black box between visits and doctor-to-doctor clinical intelligence sharing is legally and technically hard. On the patient side, the continuous longitudinal view needed for triage, diagnosis and treatment planning is missing.

What if healthcare intelligence could move, without forcing sensitive patient data to move with it?

The Solution, clinical intelligence moves, sensitive data stays
1 · Apertus basis
Swiss sovereign open-weights LLM, multilingual FR/DE/IT/dialects
→
2 · Mental-health LLM
fine-tuned and self-training on mental-health data, privacy locked
→
3 · Student edge
a shrunk model distils to clinic hardware across multiple nodes
→
4 · Airlock
PHI stays sealed, only de-identified insight crosses to other doctors

The result: clinicians get SHRUNK'ed, evidence-based, context-aware insight from other nodes while each patient record stays sealed at the local clinic and on the patient's own medium of access. The black box opens, the distance between doctor and patient shrinks, and the patient companion app adds real-time tracking plus a longitudinal triage, diagnosis and treatment-planning view.

Why Now
Swiss National EHR, the mandatory centralised EHR creates a legal crisis for psychiatrists under revFADP.
Sovereign Swiss model, Apertus is open weights, Apache 2.0, trained on the Alps supercomputer, natively multilingual FR/DE/IT/dialects; mental-health LLM and Student distillation keep intelligence local.
Doctor-to-doctor sharing, the privacy airlock makes PHI-sealed, evidence-based insight sharing between nodes possible for the first time.
Runs on existing hardware, the shrunk Student works on the machines clinics already have.
