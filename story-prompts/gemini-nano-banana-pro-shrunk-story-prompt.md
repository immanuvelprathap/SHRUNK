# SHRUNK AI — Story Prompt for Gemini Nano Banana Pro

## Recommended model & settings
- Primary: **Veo 3.1** (native audio, cinematic 8s clips) or **Sora 2 Pro** (up to 25s, HD, reference-image mode).
- Aspect ratio: **16:9**
- Total duration: **90 seconds** (render as 8–15s clips and concatenate, or use Sora 2 Pro for longer continuous motion).
- Style: **Photorealistic medical drama / cinematic motion graphics**
- Audio: **Native generated audio** (Veo 3.1) or add a separate royalty-free medical/tech soundtrack.

## Master prompt

Create a cinematic 90-second story for a Swiss sovereign clinical-intelligence platform called SHRUNK AI. The story shows how a psychiatric patient's hidden context is captured at home, transformed into structured clinical intelligence inside the clinic, and safely shared between clinicians and clinics without the patient's raw data ever leaving the building.

### Characters
- **Lena**, early 30s, anxious but resilient.
- **Dr. Keller**, psychiatrist in a Bern clinic.
- **Dr. Brenner**, crisis clinician in a Zurich hospital.

### Visual direction
- Cool Swiss palette: midnight blue, deep teal, soft orange accent.
- Clean, modern hospital and home interiors.
- Digital interfaces should look professional and readable, not sci-fi.
- Torn-paper / silo metaphor for fragmented records; a glowing timeline for the unified story.

## Scene list (for text-to-video generation)

### Scene 1 — The walk in (0:00–0:10)
**Prompt:**
> Wide shot of a woman walking down a bright but sterile hospital corridor, looking at her smartphone. On the phone screen we see a private therapy chat window with honest late-night messages. She pauses at a clinic door, then enters. Moody strings, footsteps echo. 16:9 cinematic.

### Scene 2 — The guarded consultation (0:10–0:20)
**Prompt:**
> Medium shot of Lena sitting across from a psychiatrist in a modern office. She smiles and says "I'm fine" while the doctor types notes. Split-screen reveals her phone at night with very different, honest messages. Soft teal office lighting, orange accent on the phone screen. Cinematic, realistic.

### Scene 3 — Fragmented records (0:20–0:30)
**Prompt:**
> Abstract motion graphic of four labeled silos — Clinic, Hospital, Therapy, Pharmacy — each holding a torn piece of paper. The pieces try to connect but fail. A patient icon sits in the middle, surrounded by incomplete documents. Clean vector style, dark blue background.

### Scene 4 — Information does not move (0:30–0:40)
**Prompt:**
> A referral document travels from one city to another but arrives as a faded, partial fax. A second doctor in a different office reads only a medication list, while Lena retells her story, looking tired. Realistic office interiors, cool palette.

### Scene 5 — SHRUNK arrives (0:40–0:55)
**Prompt:**
> A Swiss-made Apertus AI brain icon descends into an on-premise server room inside the clinic. Lena holds a glowing digital key and taps it to grant consent. Her journal, chat, sleep data, and clinic notes merge into one continuous glowing timeline on a screen. Hopeful music begins to build.

### Scene 6 — Clinical Intelligence Document (0:55–1:10)
**Prompt:**
> Close-up of a professional clinical dashboard screen. The following sections appear one by one, each highlighted by the cursor: CLINICAL QUESTION, RETRIEVED EVIDENCE, PATIENT-SPECIFIC SUMMARY, CLINICAL INTERPRETATION, CLINICAL EVALUATION, UNCERTAINTY OR MISSING INFORMATION, SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS, RECOMMENDED CLINICIAN REVIEW, CLINICAL WORKFLOW, SOURCES. Clean UI, dark mode with teal and orange accents.

### Scene 7 — Intelligence moves, data stays (1:10–1:25)
**Prompt:**
> The Clinical Intelligence Document flows as a sealed, consent-gated packet from Dr. Keller's screen to Dr. Brenner's screen in another city. The original patient record stays locked in the Bern clinic. Animated network lines connect clinics but the patient folder remains inside the building. Upbeat, trustworthy music.

### Scene 8 — Patient chat at home (1:25–1:40)
**Prompt:**
> Lena at home on a sofa opens the SHRUNK companion app. She types about sleep, mood, and medication side effects after a session. The app updates a longitudinal timeline. She sees the same summary her doctor sees and taps to adjust consent. Warm home lighting, calm music.

### Scene 9 — XAI + CEAI + Federation (1:40–1:55)
**Prompt:**
> Dr. Keller's screen shows three panels: XAI highlights the evidence behind each sentence, CEAI displays an alignment score, and a Federation panel shows that only anonymized insights leave the clinic. All processing is labeled "on-premise" and "PHI-sealed." Clean UI motion graphics.

### Scene 10 — Closing (1:55–2:05)
**Prompt:**
> Lena and Dr. Keller sit together, both looking at the same transparent clinical story on the screen. Final title card appears: "SHRUNK AI — the model comes to the data. Patient data never leaves the clinic." Warm, resolved, confident music.

## Music / audio direction
- Use Veo 3.1 native audio for ambient clinic sounds, footsteps, and soft UI clicks.
- Underlying score: piano and strings, building from uncertainty to trust.
- Final 10 seconds: a warm, minimal Swiss-inspired motif.
