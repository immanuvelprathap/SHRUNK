# SHRUNK AI — 60-90 Second Story Prompt for Higgsfield AI

## Tone & style
- Cinematic, human, realistic (not emoji-like).
- Medical drama with a hopeful, sovereign-tech resolution.
- Cool Swiss palette: midnight blue, teal, soft orange highlights.
- Music: understated piano/strings, building to a warm, resolved motif.

## Cast
- Lena, 30s, anxious but composed.
- Dr. Keller, middle-aged psychiatrist.
- Dr. Brenner, crisis clinician in another city.

## Scene-by-scene

### 0:00–0:08 — The walk in
A woman (Lena) walks down a hospital corridor. She looks at her phone, then at the clinic door. On her phone we see a private chat window: honest messages she typed at 2 a.m. that no clinician can see. Music is sparse, slightly tense.

### 0:08–0:16 — The fragmented context
Inside the office, Lena says "I'm fine." The doctor's screen shows scattered PDFs, silos, incomplete notes. Visual: torn pieces of paper floating between icons labeled Clinic, Hospital, Therapy, Pharmacy. None connect.

### 0:16–0:24 — Two records
Split screen: on the left, the guarded conversation in the clinic; on the right, Lena's phone and therapy journal at night, revealing the real story. The doctor has no visibility into the right side.

### 0:24–0:32 — Information does not move
A referral fax / email travels from Bern to Zurich but arrives as a fragment. Dr. Brenner reads only a medication list. Lena has to retell her story from the beginning, embarrassed and edited.

### 0:32–0:44 — SHRUNK appears
A Swiss Apertus model icon descends into the clinic server room (on-premise, not a cloud). Lena holds a digital key and taps to grant consent. Her journal, chat, sleep data, and clinic notes fuse into one continuous timeline.

### 0:44–0:56 — Clinical intelligence flows
The doctor's screen now displays a Clinical Intelligence Document with these blocks:
- CLINICAL QUESTION
- RETRIEVED EVIDENCE
- PATIENT-SPECIFIC SUMMARY
- CLINICAL INTERPRETATION
- CLINICAL EVALUATION
- UNCERTAINTY OR MISSING INFORMATION
- SAFETY CONSIDERATIONS
- RECOMMENDED CLINICIAN REVIEW
- CLINICAL WORKFLOW
- SOURCES

The same briefing travels to Dr. Brenner with Lena's consent. Doctor-to-doctor and clinic-to-clinic, the intelligence moves while the raw data stays locked in the clinic.

### 0:56–1:12 — Patient at home
Lena at home opens the SHRUNK companion chat. She logs symptoms, sleep, mood between sessions and after sessions. The timeline updates incrementally. She sees the same summary her doctor sees. She controls what is shared.

### 1:12–1:24 — XAI + CEAI + Federation
Close-up of the doctor's screen: XAI highlights the evidence behind each sentence; CEAI shows an alignment score; a Federation panel shows that only de-identified insights cross to partner clinics, never PHI. All processing stays inside the clinic.

### 1:24–1:30 — Closing line
Lena and Dr. Keller sit together, the screen between them showing the same transparent story. Final title card:

> SHRUNK AI — the model comes to the data. Patient data never leaves the clinic.

## Music direction
- Opening: solo piano, uncertain tempo.
- Middle: low strings as fragments fail to connect.
- Resolution: warm synth pad + subtle Swiss folk-inspired motif when the key turns.
- End: confident, minimal, trustworthy.

## Output settings
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Duration: 90 seconds
- Style: photorealistic illustration / cinematic motion graphics
